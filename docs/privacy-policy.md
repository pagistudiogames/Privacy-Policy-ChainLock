# LockChain Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026.

LockChain stores game settings, personal records, daily streaks and gameplay statistics locally on the user's device. LockChain does not require an account and does not operate a backend that receives this gameplay data.

LockChain uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, advertising interactions and approximate location derived from network information to provide, secure and measure ads. Where required, LockChain presents Google's consent form before requesting ads and provides access to privacy options from Settings.

LockChain offers an optional, non-consumable Google Play purchase that removes interstitial advertising. Google Play processes the transaction and supplies purchase and entitlement status to the application. LockChain does not receive or store payment-card or billing details. Purchase entitlement is cached locally so that the advertising preference remains available when the device is temporarily offline.

Challenge codes and shared results leave the device only when the user explicitly invokes the operating system's share function. The user chooses the destination and recipient.

Users can erase locally stored LockChain data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application. Advertising consent choices can be reviewed from Settings → Privacy options.

LockChain is not directed specifically at children. The final age classification and target-audience declaration are configured in the relevant app store.

Questions about this policy can be sent to [pagistudio.games+support@gmail.com](mailto:pagistudio.games+support@gmail.com).
