LockChain Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

LockChain is developed by PAGI Studio. No user account is required to play, and PAGI Studio does not operate a backend that receives gameplay data.

Advertising and consent

The free edition uses Google AdMob interstitial advertisements. Ads may appear between games, never during active gameplay. Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent and the privacy choices available in the app's Settings screen.

Data stored on your device

Settings, personal records, daily streaks and gameplay statistics are stored locally on your device. This data is used only to provide game functionality and display your progress. It can be erased by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling LockChain.

Google Play purchases

LockChain offers an optional one-time purchase that removes interstitial advertising. Google Play processes the transaction and provides purchase entitlement status to the app. PAGI Studio does not receive or store payment-card or billing details. Entitlement status may be cached locally and restored through Google Play.

Sharing

Challenge codes and game results leave the device only when you choose to use Android's share function. You select the destination and recipient.

Personal data

PAGI Studio does not sell your personal data. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, diagnostics and advertising interactions according to its own policies when AdMob or Google Play Billing is used. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

LockChain is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, its services or legal requirements change. The date above will identify the latest version.
